# Glossary

The words every Gauth product uses for the same thing. One thing has one name, in every product and in each language. The strings live in `src/core/messages.ts`; this page says what each one means and what not to say instead.

## Skill levels

One ladder, lowest first. A level describes a skill for one student, from the evidence so far.

| Term        | Spanish       | Meaning                                                | Do not say                |
| ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Not started | Sin empezar   | No evidence yet. It is not a level a student earned.   | Unknown, None, 0%         |
| Attempted   | Intentado     | The student tried and has not shown the skill yet.     | Failed, Struggling, Weak  |
| Familiar    | Familiarizado | Right with help, or right some of the time.            | Developing, Basic         |
| Proficient  | Competente    | Right without help, most of the time.                  | Passing, Good             |
| Mastered    | Dominado      | Right without help, each time, across problem types.   | Perfect, Expert, 100%     |

## Answers and feedback

| Term           | Spanish            | Meaning                                                                 | Do not say                  |
| -------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Not yet        | Todavía no         | The answer is not right. The student can try again.                     | Wrong, Incorrect, Error     |
| Correct        | Correcto           | The answer is right.                                                    | Perfect, Great job          |
| Your answer    | Tu respuesta       | What the student picked or wrote.                                       | Wrong answer                |
| Correct answer | Respuesta correcta | The right answer, shown after the student's own.                        | Solution, Key               |
| Hint           | Pista              | One step of help before the answer. It does not give the answer.        | Tip, Help                   |
| Why            | Por qué            | The reason an answer is right, shown after the student answers.         | Explanation, Solution       |
| Try again      | Inténtalo de nuevo | The action after "Not yet".                                             | Retry, Redo                 |

## Numbers

| Term      | Form          | Meaning                                                              | Do not say   |
| --------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| pts       | +8 pts        | A change in a share, in points.                                      | +8%          |
| n of N    | 2 of 6        | Progress through a set. Spanish: "2 de 6".                           | 2/6, 33%     |
| Day       | Fri, Oct 23   | A date near today. Spanish follows US order: "vie, 23 oct".          | 10/23        |

## Product words

| Term     | Meaning                                                          | Do not say            |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Gauth AI | The assistant. It is named where its output appears.             | The AI, Bot, Copilot  |
| Skill    | One thing a student can learn to do, tied to a standard.         | Topic, Concept, KP    |
| Standard | A state or national code for a skill, such as HSG.SRT.B.5.       | Code, Tag             |
| Set      | The problems a student works through in one sitting.             | Quiz, Test            |

The Spanish strings need a review by a native speaker who teaches in a US classroom before a product ships them.
