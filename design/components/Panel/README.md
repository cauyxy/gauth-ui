# Panel

The white card staff pages are built from. `Panel` and `PanelHeader` in `src/components/Panel.tsx`.

- 16px radius, white, `shadow-edge` (a 1px inset hairline), no drop shadow.
- `PanelHeader` is at least 64px high with an 18px/600 title and optional actions on the right.
- A page has one hero at most; everything else is a panel.
